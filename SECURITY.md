# Security Policy


## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover any vulnerabilities please email: joejoekitch@gmail.com | All emails will be read within 2 - 4 Business Days. 

When reporting a vulnerability please make sure to contain your Username and the Email you wish for a response to be sent to. Responses could take up to 3 - 5 Business Days. Thank you for your time, please be patient.
